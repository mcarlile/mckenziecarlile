Product Design

Amazon

A browser extension concept helping Prime members discover the full value of their subscription — surfacing relevant music, video, and book content exactly when they're searching for it.

Role UX Design Intern
Timeline Summer 2014
Team Browser Integration Technology

Amazon Prime offered a growing suite of digital benefits — Prime Music, Kindle Lending Library, Amazon Instant Video — but many subscribers didn't know what they had access to, let alone how to use it. The existing 1Button browser extension handled price comparisons for physical goods, but left the entire media side of Prime untouched. My internship project explored how to change that.

The problem

Prime is a compelling subscription on paper, but its value is easy to underestimate when benefits are scattered across disconnected surfaces. A member searching for a book, movie, or album elsewhere on the web has no signal that they might already have access through Prime. The opportunity was clear: meet members where they already are, at the moment of intent, and show them what's already theirs.

"Leverage Amazon's digital media services to increase the value of a Prime subscription — and surface surprisingly relevant, accurate contextual suggestions."

Process

Mapping the use cases

I started by mapping user scenarios across the three primary content types — books, video, and music — and across different web contexts where searches happen. Each context warranted different extension behavior and content prioritization. The extension also needed to handle account state: a member who already owns a title sees something different than one who hasn't purchased it.

Wireframes

With use cases defined, I worked through wireframes covering the range of trigger contexts and content types. The design challenge was keeping the extension lightweight and non-intrusive — helpful when relevant, invisible when not.

Prototype

I applied Amazon's established visual design language to bring the wireframes to a prototype-ready state, testing across the primary content type scenarios.

Outcome

The project aimed to boost Prime awareness, increase conversion, and drive engagement with underutilized digital benefits. The prototype was approved for public sharing by my internship managers. Specific shipment details are covered by NDA.
