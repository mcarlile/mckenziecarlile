Product Design

Waymo

Designing the rider experience for the world's most advanced autonomous vehicle service — from app to in-vehicle interactions.

Role Product Designer
Focus Autonomous vehicle experience

Waymo's mission is to be the world's most trusted driver. While working as a designer here, I design and ship internal tools across a wide variety of technical domains. These products not only enabled Waymo to safely deploy the Waymo Driver, but enable the company to execute on this mission at scale:

  • Simulation and evaluation
  • Dataset management
  • Data mining
  • Event triage, annotations and labeling

It's been an honor watching Waymo scale from a science project serving just a handful of trusted testers in suburban phoenix to a robust business serving XXX rides in XX markets across the world.

In addition to serving as an embedded UX designer and researcher, I helped bootstrap and co-lead our organizations's Design and Data visualization system, SoHo. Although it started as a passion project on the side, I helped our design team advocate for and secure dedicated design and engineering headcount for this effort.
